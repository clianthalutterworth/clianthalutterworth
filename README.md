# Cliantha Lutterworth

I’m a business writer and content planner based in York, UK. I turn practical experience into clear features, useful contributor briefs and realistic publishing plans. My work brings together business feature writing, content creation, editorial planning and blog community management.

I began in editorial support in 1982, organising submissions, checking references and preparing short business profiles. Through Lutterworth Editorial, I later focused on accessible features and guidance for contributors. My Plain Steps project brought practical checklists and case studies to people starting community publications.

Today I prepare interview-led articles, organise manageable schedules and help contributors take part in thoughtful discussions. Since 2021, my Small Changes Desk project has explored the modest decisions that improve everyday working routines. I prefer specific questions, careful notes and time for revision to promises of instant results.

Away from the desk, I enjoy bookbinding, walks beside the River Ouse, photographing old shopfronts and visiting independent bookshops. I was born in 1960. You can find my business stories and practical explanations at https://fixnewstips.com/.
